Who we are

Business infrastructure, not another marketplace listing.

Finance & government schemes

Financing matched to district producers

Based on typical eligibility for registered district MSMEs and artisans. Final eligibility depends on your documentation and lender assessment.

Collateral-free MSME loans Working capital

Loans up to ₹2 crore without collateral, routed through partner banks and NBFCs for GST-registered producers and cooperatives.

Micro-enterprise credit Up to ₹10L

Small-ticket loans for individual artisans and micro-units, tiered by business stage — from starting out to scaling production.

Artisan & craftsperson support scheme Toolkits + credit

Recognition, skill certification and starter toolkit assistance for traditional trade and craft producers.

First-time entrepreneur credit Women / SC-ST priority

Bank-facilitated loans for setting up a new greenfield enterprise, with priority processing for women and first-generation entrepreneurs.

State ODOP incentive grants Varies by state

Subsidies on branding, packaging and common facility centres, allocated by your state's ODOP nodal department.
